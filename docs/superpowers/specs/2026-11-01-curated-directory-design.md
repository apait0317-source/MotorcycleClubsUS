# Curated directory and useful rider content

The request is to review additional monetization and improve content after an AdSense quality rejection. The user selected curation and explicitly asked us to use judgment and skip further design questions. This authorizes local implementation, not publication or account changes.

## Evidence and approach

Baseline is current `main` at `17594e9`, not the older initial workspace. It has 452 promoted profiles, including 240 without descriptions and 265 failing even the existing lenient description heuristic. Only 74 have reviewed, source-backed descriptions. Raising the numeric score alone leaves 17 description-free profiles at score five or higher. Three promoted records still have UK addresses and US city labels. Existing guides already have official references and must retain those improvements.

Continuing enrichment across the entire footprint was considered; the prior pass already researched weak entries and many lack usable sources. Revenue-first expansion adds promotion without fixing reader value. Selected approach: curate publication around reviewed original information, retain basic discovery entries as clearly limited information on hubs, and improve existing guides with usable decision aids.

## Publication and browsing

- Preserve raw records, stable profile slugs, chapter exclusions and reviewed overlays.
- A promoted/indexable/ad-eligible profile must be a confirmed club with an approved description, attributed description evidence and at least the existing detail score. A photo, rating or long unsourced label cannot establish editorial value. This is an internal editorial rule, not Google's approval formula.
- Basic unreviewed entries remain usable on state hubs as limited entries with official outbound links where available; no standalone internal promotion, sitemap inclusion, ads or affiliate shopping blocks on their thin profile pages.
- City hubs need two reviewed profiles; state hubs need two reviewed profiles for indexing and ads. Routes remain reachable. Show reviewed summaries on promoted cards so hubs offer an actual comparison.
- Exclude confirmed foreign organizations and non-club publications through evidenced overlays. Do not infer closure from a failed fetch. Do not add or relicense photos; existing usage permission is an operator-supplied assumption recorded by the prior review.
- Homepage leads with source-reviewed profiles and useful guides, not photo/rating-led endorsement. Counts clearly distinguish reviewed profiles from basic directory entries. Use actual data, never a hardcoded public count.
- Audit all eligible real non-chapter records, including high-score entries with no supported description and entries that lose indexability after curation. Numeric scores remain historical diagnostics, not publication decisions.

## Content and monetization

Improve existing joining, verification and group-riding guides with a comparison worksheet, first-contact template, verification record and practical ride-planning prompts. Keep claims within cited sources and distinguish suggested questions from organization-specific rules. No fabricated experience, credentials, prices, universal membership rules or product testing.

Use guide-specific affiliate choices near a relevant section, replacing the repeated generic guide sidebar. Shopping-category links must not imply that arbitrary Amazon search results have verified certifications or materials. Preserve disclosures and sponsored link attributes. Club basic listings and corrections stay free. Record quarterly sponsorship and organizer downloads as opportunities requiring demand and traffic evidence; do not build checkout, accounts or a sponsorship system now.
